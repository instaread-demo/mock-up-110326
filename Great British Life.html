Six-bedroom property in West Kirby for sale at £1m

The house has stunning views of the Dee Estuary (Image: Dee Distinct Estate Agents)
By Jack Lyons Reporter

A unique West Kirby home made famous by Channel 4's Grand Designs is going on the market, offering buyers a chance to own one of Wirral's most recognisable modern houses.

The striking six-bedroom property at 104 Grange Road has far-reaching views across the Dee and over the rooftops of West Kirby.

Dee Distinct Estate Agents described it as a "remarkable architectural home" in one of Wirral's most sought-after coastal locations.

Stuart and Rosie Treasurer appeared on Series 17, Episode 8 of Grand Designs in 2016 (Image: Channel 4)

Stuart and Rosie Treasurer appeared on Grand Designs in 2016 as they transformed their dormer bungalow into a radically different family home, setting themselves a budget of £175,000 to realise their plans.

The couple, who owned wood workshop Cleancut Wood, wanted something very different from a conventional bungalow extension.

They worked with Bromilow Architects on an ambitious design which involved removing the existing roof and adding a large cantilevered timber box above the original ground floor.

A model of the project which was executed on Grand Designs (Image: Dee Distinct Estate Agents)

This house was featured on Channel 4's Grand Designs in 2016 (Image: Dee Distinct Estate Agents)

The transformation created an ultra-modern home characterised by open spaces, natural light and a raw, industrial style.

Grand Designs presenter Kevin McCloud travelled to West Kirby during the project to follow Stuart and Rosie's progress.

(Image: Dee Distinct Estate Agents)

(Image: Dee Distinct Estate Agents)

While its distinctly modern appearance attracted some reservations locally when it was first built, the property became memorable enough for Grand Designs to return to Grange Road almost a decade later.

McCloud caught up with Stuart and Rosie for a follow up episode broadcast on Channel 4 on November 5 last year, to see how the distinctive timber-clad house had changed since viewers first saw it and how it had settled into its surroundings.

(Image: Channel 4)

Now the house is looking for its next owner, with Dee Distinct Estate Agents highlighting its "Grand Designs heritage" as well as an enviable position within easy walking distance of the centre of West Kirby.

The property now has six bedrooms, four of which have en-suite facilities, as well as two substantial reception rooms and an open-plan kitchen and dining area, creating plenty of space for both everyday family life and entertaining.

(Image: Dee Distinct Estate Agents)

(Image: Dee Distinct Estate Agents)

One bedroom is located on the ground floor and could also be used as a guest suite, home office or additional living space, giving the next owner some flexibility in how the sizeable property is used.

(Image: Dee Distinct Estate Agents)

Large areas of glazing, sliding doors and strategically positioned skylights bring natural light into the property throughout the day, while a number of rooms look out across the Dee Estuary and West Kirby.

(Image: Dee Distinct Estate Agents)

The estate agent says the design means the views form an "integral part" of living in the house, particularly as the sun sets across the Dee Estuary.

(Image: Dee Distinct Estate Agents)

Outside, the property has a low-maintenance garden which receives plenty of sunshine and provides space for outdoor entertaining, while a generous driveway offers parking for several vehicles.

(Image: Dee Distinct Estate Agents)

(Image: Dee Distinct Estate Agents)

The house is within walking distance of West Kirby town centre and railway station, with access to the beach and Marine Lake as well as a wide variety of bars, restaurants and cafes.

The property is on the market chain-free. Stevie Martin, director of Dee Distinct, specialises in the sale of high value homes across the region.

He describes the house as a "truly unique property - one that provides both space to entertain and space to reflect".
